💡 教學重點與本學期課程目標 | Core Focus & Semester Objectives

【從探索世界到知識運用，建立邏輯寫作與完整表達能力】

延續 A1 班的探索基礎，Advance A2 班將帶領孩子邁入「知識活用與段落寫作」的新階段。本學期教學重點在於幫助孩子們將零散的單字與句型，轉化為有條理的書面表達與口語敘述。透過 Oxford Discover 2 跨學科主題，擴充科學、數學與歷史領域的閱讀深度；同時結合 Azar Basic Grammar 系統化語法架構，讓孩子明確理解時態對比與空間句型，培養長篇閱讀的專注力，並能自信寫出完整的短文段落。

【Expand Your Mind, Master Your Expression】 Building upon foundational skills, A2 students move from basic exploration to active knowledge application and structured writing. By combining interdisciplinary themes with systematic grammar practice, students develop reading stamina and learn to articulate their ideas in clear, cohesive paragraphs.

🌍【主題探究單元】Oxford Discover 2 (Units 1-8)

Part 1: Interdisciplinary Topics & Reading Skills

  • Unit 1: Amazing Animals 🐾 —— 探究神奇動物的生存本領與身體構造。
    Discovering unique animal traits and body adaptations.
  • Unit 2: Leo and Lily's Adventure 📖 —— 閱讀冒險故事，分析故事角色與情節發展。
    Analyzing plot, setting, and character actions in adventure literature.
  • Unit 3: What is Our World Made of? 🔬 —— 理解物質三態（固態、液態、氣態）的科學觀念。
    Understanding the science of solids, liquids, and gases.
  • Unit 4: Let's Make Ice Cream! 🍦 —— 學習製作過程的步驟說明與實驗變化。
    Learning sequence, instructions, and practical science in making ice cream.
  • Unit 5: Then and Now 📜 —— 對比過去與現在的生活演進，練習時間軸的表達。
    Comparing past and present lifestyles using historical timelines.
  • Unit 6: Tell Me a Story, Grandpa 👵 —— 傾聽傳統故事，學習故事敘述與世代經驗傳承。
    Developing narrative listening skills through family and traditional stories.
  • Unit 7: Subtraction 🧮 —— 結合基礎數學減法概念的美語應用與問題解決。
    Applying English in mathematical reasoning and subtraction word problems.
  • Unit 8: Bandar, the Greedy Monkey 🐒 —— 閱讀經典寓言故事，討論行為後果與選擇。
    Reading classic fables and discussing moral themes and choices.
